# PRD — Industrial IoT Predictive Maintenance Platform

## Product Goal
Enable manufacturing teams to monitor equipment health, predict failures early, and execute maintenance workflows with minimal downtime.

## Primary Users
- Plant managers
- Maintenance engineers
- Operations leaders

## MVP Scope
- Equipment monitoring and status tracking
- Sensor telemetry ingestion and realtime visibility
- Predictive health scoring and alerts
- Maintenance schedules and work order lifecycle
- Downtime tracking and reliability analytics (MTBF/MTTR/Uptime)
- Multi-facility support with RBAC

## Out of Scope (Current MVP)
- Advanced ML forecasting models
- External CMMS integrations
- Multi-tenant billing workflows

## Success Criteria
- Role-based workflows function end-to-end
- Facility-level data isolation enforced
- Dashboards populate with seed/demo data
- No critical security or runtime issues
